Vilano South GR CRM — simple terms

This software is provided for the private use of its licensed operators
to track government-relations contacts, interactions, events, projects,
and documents.

- Your data lives on your own computer, in the app's data folder.
  Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.
- Keep your sign-in private. Only email addresses you approve can sign in.
- Uninstalling asks before touching your data; your records are kept
  unless you explicitly choose to delete them.

No warranty is provided. If something breaks, reinstalling the latest
setup program over the existing install repairs the app without
touching your data.
